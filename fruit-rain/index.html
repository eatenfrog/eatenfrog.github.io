Fruit Rain

A free fruit-merge puzzle game by Eaten Frog, for Android on Google Play.

Available on
  • Google Play

Fruits fall into a box. Drop two of the same fruit onto each other and they merge into a bigger one: cherries become strawberries, strawberries become grapes, and so on up to the watermelon. Chain merges for combos, keep the pile below the line, and beat your best score.

Features

  • Ten fruits to merge, from cherry to watermelon.
  • Combos, player levels and daily tasks.
  • Power-ups such as bomb, shake and grow when the box gets full.
  • Fruit faces and animated themes to collect with in-game coins.
  • A weekly and all-time ranking with weekly rewards, Google Play Games achievements and cloud save of your progress.
  • Free to play, with ads. Optional in-app purchases through Google Play: No Ads, x2 Coins, Premium, Starter Pack, coin packs, Power Pack and Charm Pack.

Google account data

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. If you sign in, Fruit Rain uses your Play Games player ID and gamer name to show your name in the game, sends your achievements to Google Play Games, and keeps a backup of your game progress in your Google account's hidden app data space for this game, so you can restore it on a new phone. This data goes only between your phone and Google. If you also choose to join the game's ranking, your player ID, gamer name and scores go to our ranking server, and other players see your gamer name and scores. We never sell this data or use it for advertising.

Purchases

Purchases are optional and are made with Google Play's billing system. Google Play handles the payment, and we never see your card or other payment details. No Ads, x2 Coins, Premium and Starter Pack stay with your Google account and come back if you reinstall the game. Refunds go through Google Play.

Privacy
