Fruit Rain

A fruit-merge puzzle game by Eaten Frog, for Android and the web.

Fruits fall into a box. Drop two of the same fruit onto each other and they merge into a bigger one: cherries become strawberries, strawberries become grapes, and so on up to the watermelon. Chain merges for combos, keep the pile below the line, and beat your best score.

Features

  • Ten fruits to merge, from cherry to watermelon.
  • Combos, player levels and daily tasks.
  • Power-ups such as bomb, shake and grow when the box gets full.
  • Fruit faces and animated themes to collect with in-game coins.
  • On Android: Google Play Games leaderboards with weekly rewards, achievements and cloud save of your progress.
  • No real-money purchases.

Google account data

On Android, signing in to Google Play Games is optional. If you sign in, Fruit Rain uses your Play Games player ID and gamer name to show your name and your place on the leaderboards, sends your scores and achievements to Google Play Games, and keeps a backup of your game progress in your Google account's hidden app data space for this game, so you can restore it on a new phone. This data goes only between your phone and Google. We never receive it, sell it or use it for advertising.
