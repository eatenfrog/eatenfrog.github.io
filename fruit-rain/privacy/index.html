Fruit Rain Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Fruit Rain is a game made by Eaten Frog, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is used when you play Fruit Rain on Android (Google Play) or on the web.

In short: Fruit Rain has no accounts and we have no server. We do not ask for your name or email address. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress, coins, purchases made with in-game coins, settings and the birth year you enter on the age screen are saved only on your device. We never receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

2. Age screen

On Android, the game asks for your birth year the first time you play. We use it only on your device to choose the right ad settings. Google receives only an age setting, for example that ads must be suitable for children, never the year itself.

  • Players under 13 get ads treated as directed to children: no personalized ads and only content rated suitable for all ages (G).
  • Players under 18 get Google's teen treatment, and no player sees ads rated above PG.
  • Players under 16 in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are not asked for ad consent and do not get personalized ads.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Fruit Rain shows full-screen ads between games and optional reward videos. Watching a reward video is always your choice. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data of the ad software.

Ads are personalized only where the law allows it and you have agreed. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form from Google is shown when you first play. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your phone's settings under Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Notifications

On Android, the game can remind you once a day that your daily gift is ready. The reminder is scheduled on your device and sends no data to anyone. The game asks for permission first, and you can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

5. Web version

If you play Fruit Rain on a web game site such as CrazyGames or Yandex Games, that site shows the ads and may save your progress, and its own privacy policy applies.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or sell personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services; Unity Analytics and Unity crash reporting are turned off.
  • The game has no chat, no social features and no in-app purchases with real money.

7. Children

Fruit Rain is made for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The age screen makes sure children only get ads that follow Google's rules for children and families.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. To delete what is saved on your device, uninstall the game or clear its data. For data held by Google, use the controls in your Google account or contact Google. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK) you may have further rights; you can always contact us about them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
