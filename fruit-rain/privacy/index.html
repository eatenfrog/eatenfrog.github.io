Fruit Rain Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Fruit Rain is a game made by Eaten Frog, an individual game developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the game under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). This policy explains what information is used when you play Fruit Rain on Android (Google Play) or on the web.

In short: Fruit Rain has no accounts of its own and we run no servers, so we do not receive your game data. Your progress stays on your device. On Android you can also back it up to your Google account with Google Play Games. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads. Players under 13 never get personalized ads.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress, coins, items bought with in-game coins, settings, the in-game name you type and the birth year you enter on the age screen are saved on your device. We never receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data, unless you back them up with Google Play Games (section 4). Android's own device backup, if you have it turned on, may also copy the game's saved data to your Google account and bring it back on a new phone; Google handles that backup, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

2. Age screen

On Android, the game asks for your birth year the first time you play, before any ad is loaded. The game never sends the year anywhere (only Android's device backup, if turned on, copies it with the rest of the game's data). From the year alone, the game takes the youngest age you could be, so a player whose birthday is still ahead this year gets the younger group's treatment. The game only tells Google which ad treatment to use:

  • Players under 13: ads are treated as directed to children. The advertising ID is not sent, no personalized ads or remarketing are shown, and only ads rated for all ages (G) appear.
  • Players from 13 to 17: Google's teen treatment applies, and no player sees ads rated above PG.
  • Players under 16 in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are not asked for ad consent and do not get personalized ads.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Fruit Rain shows full-screen ads between games and optional reward videos. Watching a reward video is always your choice. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google collects:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID (the advertising ID is never sent for players under 13),
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be estimated,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads, and performance data of the ad software.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown after the age screen to players aged 16 and over, and ads are personalized only if you agree. Elsewhere, Google may personalize ads for players aged 18 and over unless you turn this off. For players from 13 to 17, Google's teen protections apply (section 2). You can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy in the game, where it is offered, or in your phone's settings under Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads), where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID.

Google acts as an independent company for this data. How Google uses it: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Google Play Games (Android)

On Android, Fruit Rain can use Google Play Games Services, which Google provides. When you are signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, the game uses this Google user data:

  • your Play Games player ID and gamer name, to show your name in the game and on leaderboards,
  • your scores, sent to Google's leaderboards so you can compare them with other players. The game also loads the top scores, gamer names and Play Games player IDs of other players, and your place on the weekly board, to show the ranking and give weekly rewards,
  • your achievements, stored by Google in your Play Games profile,
  • a copy of your game progress (coins, level, unlocked looks, statistics, your in-game name), saved as a saved game in your Google account's hidden app data space for this game, so you can get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling. The game updates it when you sign in, finish a game, open the main menu or come back to the game, and merges it with the progress on your phone so your records, levels and unlocked items from both are kept. The birth year from the age screen is not part of it. The game can only see its own saved game, never your other files.

This data goes directly between your phone and Google; we do not receive it. The game uses Google user data only to provide these game features. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, transfer it to anyone else, or let any person read it. Signing in is optional, and the game works fully without it. For children with a supervised Google account, Google and the parent's Google Family Link settings decide whether Play Games can be used. Fruit Rain's use of information received from Google Play Games Services follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Other Google Play features

  • Rating window: the game may ask you to rate it in Google Play's own rating window. A rating or review you choose to give goes to Google Play and is published under Google Play's terms. We only see it as a public review.
  • Updates: the game asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. Google Play handles the download.
  • Time check: on Android, to give weekly leaderboard rewards fairly, the game reads the current time from Google's servers (www.google.com), whether or not you are signed in to Play Games. Like any web request, this sends your IP address and standard technical headers to Google. The game sends no game or personal data with it and keeps nothing from it except the time.

6. Notifications

On Android, the game can remind you that your daily gift is ready, at most once a day and only while the game is closed. The reminder is scheduled on your device and sends no data to anyone. On Android 13 and newer, the game asks for permission after you collect your first daily gift. On older versions, Android allows notifications by default. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

7. Web version

If you play Fruit Rain on a web game site such as CrazyGames or Yandex Games, that site shows the ads, may save your progress to its own account system, and its own privacy policy applies. Your progress is otherwise kept in your browser's storage. If you are signed in to CrazyGames, the game may use your CrazyGames username as your default in-game name. It does not use your CrazyGames picture or account ID.

8. What we do not do

  • We run no servers and receive no game, device or advertising data. The data described in sections 3 to 5 is handled by Google.
  • We do not sell personal information for money.
  • We use no analytics or crash reporting services of our own, and Unity's analytics, diagnostics and crash reporting are turned off in the game.
  • The game has no chat and no purchases with real money. Other players can see only your Play Games profile (gamer name and picture, as your Play Games privacy settings allow) and your scores on the leaderboards.

9. Children

Fruit Rain is made for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game never asks for a real name, email address or any contact information. The optional in-game name you can type stays on your device and, if you use Play Games, in your own cloud save (sections 1 and 4). For players under 13, the game tells Google to treat every ad request as directed to children (section 2). Google then does not use the advertising ID and shows no personalized ads or remarketing. It may still use limited technical information, such as the IP address and device information, only to support internal operations: to show ads based on the game's content, limit how often an ad appears, measure ads and prevent fraud. Only ad software certified for Google Play's Families program is used. A parent who has questions, or wants information connected with their child removed, can contact us (section 14).

10. Why this information may be used (legal bases)

  • Personalized ads, and storing or reading ad information on your device where the law requires consent: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1)). You can withdraw it at any time as described in section 3.
  • Non-personalized ads, ad measurement, frequency limits and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free game and keeping ads safe (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Play Games sign-in, leaderboards, achievements and cloud save: providing the features you chose to use (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Answering your emails: our legitimate interest in replying to you.

11. Where the information goes

Google processes the data described in sections 3 to 5 on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google describes the safeguards it uses for these transfers, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses, in its privacy policy.

12. How long information is kept, and how to delete it

  • On your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data (Android: Settings > Apps > Fruit Rain > Storage > Clear storage).
  • Play Games data (cloud save, scores, achievements): until you delete it. In the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon > Settings > General > Play Games Profile > Privacy & Settings > Your Data > Delete profile & game data, then under "Delete individual game data" find Fruit Rain and tap Delete. On the web, go to play.google.com/games/profile > Your Data > Delete Play Games Account & Data. You can also delete your whole Play Games profile there. Clear the game's data on your phone first (see above). Otherwise the game saves your progress to Play Games again the next time it syncs. Deleting a game's data may not remove scores already on a leaderboard; deleting your whole Play Games profile removes them.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google as described in its privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and manage your Google ad data at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer you, and deleted within 12 months.

If you cannot delete something yourself, email us (section 14) and we will help within 30 days.

13. Security

All data the game and its Google services send over the internet is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Data saved on your device is protected by your device's own app sandbox. We hold no copy of it.

14. Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Under KVKK Article 11 this includes the right to learn whether your data is processed and to whom it is transferred, and to ask for its correction or deletion. Because we hold no personal data about you except emails you send us, most requests are handled by Google through your Google account controls. We will gladly help with any request. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, or in Türkiye to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK, kvkk.gov.tr).

U.S. residents: we do not sell personal information for money. Google's personalized ads may count as "selling" or "sharing" under some U.S. state privacy laws. You can opt out under Settings > Privacy in the game where that option is shown, and at any time by turning off ad personalization in your phone's settings.
