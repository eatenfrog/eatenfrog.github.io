Delete your Dr.Math data

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Dr.Math is a game by Eaten Frog for Android (Google Play, package name com.eatenfrog.drmath) and for web browsers on CrazyGames. The game has no accounts of its own. The only data we keep on a server is the leaderboard data of players who signed in to Google Play Games or to a CrazyGames account and joined the leaderboard. This page explains how to have it deleted, and how to delete the data that Google and your phone keep. More detail is in the Dr.Math Privacy Policy.

1. Leaderboard data (kept by us)

To delete your leaderboard data, email eatenfrog.gamedev@gmail.com with the subject "Dr.Math data deletion" and write:

  • your Play Games gamer name, or on CrazyGames your CrazyGames username, as it appears on the Dr.Math leaderboard,
  • roughly when you last played, if you remember.

We may ask one short question to make sure the request comes from the player (or a parent of a child player). Within 30 days we permanently delete:

  • your player record: Play Games player ID and gamer name (or CrazyGames user ID and username), level points and best scores,
  • every game result you sent (score, correct answers, longest combo, play time, date),
  • your weekly scores and the records of the weekly rewards you took.

We keep nothing about you afterwards, except your email for up to 12 months so we can answer follow-up questions. Abuse-prevention counters by IP address are not linked to a player and delete themselves within about two days. If you keep playing while signed in to Play Games and joined to the leaderboard, a new record starts with your next game.

2. Play Games cloud save and achievements (kept by Google)

Your progress backup (XP, coins, owned themes and other progress) and your achievements are stored by Google in your Play Games profile. You can delete them yourself:

  1. First clear the game's data on your phone (section 3); otherwise the game saves your progress to Play Games again the next time it syncs.
  2. In the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon > Settings > General > Play Games Profile > Privacy & Settings > Your Data > Delete profile & game data.
  3. Under "Delete individual game data", find Dr.Math and tap Delete.

On the web you can do the same at play.google.com/games/profile > Your Data. Deleting this data also removes the coins and items saved in it, and it cannot be undone.

3. Data on your phone

Uninstall the game, or go to Settings > Apps > Dr.Math > Storage > Clear storage. This deletes your progress, settings, privacy answer, birth year and purchase records from the phone. Coins bought while not signed in to Play Games are lost with it.

4. Saved progress on CrazyGames

On CrazyGames, your progress is saved by CrazyGames: with your CrazyGames account if you are signed in, otherwise in your browser. Clear the site data for crazygames.com in your browser to delete a guest's progress; for progress kept with your account, use your CrazyGames account settings or contact CrazyGames. CrazyGames' privacy policy: crazygames.com/privacy-policy.

5. Purchases and ads

  • Google Play keeps your purchase history in your Google account under its own policy; we keep order records only as long as accounting and tax laws require.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings and manage your ad data at myadcenter.google.com.
