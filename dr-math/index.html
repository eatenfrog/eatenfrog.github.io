Dr.Math

A free quick-decision math game by Eaten Frog, for Android on Google Play.

Available on
  • Google Play

Two operations appear on the screen, like 37 + 58 and 46 + 51. Tap the one with the larger result before the timer runs out. You don't need the exact answer, only a quick, good guess.

You start with one-digit addition. Five correct answers in a row move you on to subtraction, then multiplication, then division, and after division the numbers get one digit longer. The timer gets shorter every time you change tier. At five digits the operations are mixed and the game goes on until you slip.

Features

  • Three modes: Classic, Competitive (opens when you first reach tier 5) and Practice, where you choose the operation and the number of digits and there is no timer.
  • Levels from Student to Professor, earned with XP in Classic and Competitive.
  • A worldwide leaderboard of correct answers at five digits, Google Play Games achievements and cloud save of your progress.
  • Played by tapping, in landscape, in 10 languages.
  • Free to play, with ads. One optional in-app purchase through Google Play: Remove Ads.

Google account data

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. If you are signed in, Dr.Math uses your Play Games player ID and gamer name, sends your achievements to Google Play Games, and keeps a copy of your progress in your Google account's hidden app data space for this game. It also sends the results of your Classic and Competitive games to our leaderboard server, where other players see your gamer name and your total. We never sell this data or use it for advertising.

Purchases

Remove Ads is optional and is bought with Google Play's billing system. Google Play handles the payment, and we never see your card or other payment details. The purchase stays with your Google account and comes back if you reinstall the game. Refunds go through Google Play.
